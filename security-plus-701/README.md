| 3. Security architecture | 18% | 16 |

| 5. Security program management and oversight | 20% | 18 |

## Using it

Open the [Field Notes site](https://kathan2004.github.io/Notes/#papers), where the practice bank sits alongside the notes chapters.

- **Dashboard:** estimated score weighted by exam domain, domain mastery, questions to revisit and study streak.
- **Full papers:** exam mode (timed at 90 minutes, results at the end) or practice mode (answer and explanation after each question).
- **Drills:** domain drills, missed questions, bookmarks, Quick 20, and custom sessions filtered by paper, domain and unseen questions.
- **In a question:** cross out options, flag, bookmark, and keyboard shortcuts (A-E or 1-5 select, Enter check/next, arrows move, F flag, S bookmark).
- **Reference:** acronym flashcards, a common ports table and the risk formulas.
- **Search:** across all 900 questions, options and explanations, with a one-click drill on the matches.
- **Progress:** session history, text size, and a progress code to move your progress between devices.

The raw questions are in `papers/p01.json` to `papers/p10.json`. Each item is `[domain, question, options, answer, explanation]`, where `answer` is a 0-based index, or a list of indices for "Choose TWO" questions.

## Note

All questions are original practice material written against the published SY0-701 exam objectives. They are not actual exam questions. The scaled score is an approximation, since CompTIA does not publish its scoring method.
